About this estimate

This tool models the Local Governmental Employees' Retirement System (LGERS) — the plan covering Town of Cary employees — using the 1.85% accrual formula, the published early-retirement reduction tables for general employees, and the vesting and eligibility rules described in the LGERS Employer Handbook and My NC Retirement's benefit-calculation pages. It is built independently and is not produced, reviewed, or endorsed by the NC Department of State Treasurer, the Retirement Systems Division, or the Town of Cary.

It estimates the maximum allowance (single-life annuity with no survivor benefit) by default, with an optional comparison against Options 2, 3, 6-2 and 6-3. Those survivor-option figures are a standard actuarial approximation — a public unisex mortality table plus a discount rate calibrated to match LGERS's own published worked example — not the Retirement System's actual internal factor tables, which aren't published. It does not model Option 4 (Social Security Leveling), disability retirement, Discontinued Service Retirement, agricultural extension service, multiple-account members, or the IRS Section 415(b) or contribution-based benefit cap. Fractional-year reduction percentages are estimated by proportionally interpolating the published tables and may not exactly match the Retirement System's own calculation near a birthday. LGERS deferred (vested) benefits do not accrue interest or automatic cost-of-living increases while deferred; any COLA is granted ad hoc by the General Assembly and is never guaranteed, and none is assumed here.

Use this to compare scenarios, not to file paperwork. Before making a retirement decision, request an official estimate through your ORBIT account or from NC Retirement Systems (NC.Retirement@nctreasurer.gov, 919-814-4590), and consider talking with a financial advisor — this isn't financial or legal advice.
